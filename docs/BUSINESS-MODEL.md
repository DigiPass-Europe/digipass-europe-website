# Financial model (proposal)

Everything below is a proposal for the Board and the General Assembly. Figures are planning assumptions, not adopted decisions.

## Revenue lines and their statutory basis (§ 3 b)

| Line | Statutes | Nature | Year-1 assumption |
|---|---|---|---|
| Membership fees | § 3 b 1 | core, recurring | 40 organisations × avg €1,100 + 80 individuals × avg €140 ≈ €55k |
| Strategic / founding partnerships | § 3 b 1, 4 | fee tier + sponsorship package | 4 × €5,000 = €20k |
| Event participation fees | § 3 b 5 | conference, workshops | €25k |
| Exhibition, stands, advertising rights | § 3 b 13–14 | sponsorship | €15k |
| Training Academy | § 3 a 2–3, § 3 b 5 | paid courses, member discount | €10k |
| Research and project income | § 3 b 6 | Horizon Europe / Digital Europe, contracts | variable; target 1 CSA participation |
| Publications | § 3 b 11 | reports sold to non-members | marginal |
| Subsidies, donations, crowdfunding | § 3 b 2–3, 16 | one-off | not budgeted |

## Three things that must stay separate
1. **Membership fees** buy membership and its statutory rights (votes per § 10 g). They never buy visibility beyond the directory.
2. **Sponsorship** (events, exhibition, advertising rights) buys visibility. It confers no governance rights (§ 10 g caps votes at 4 regardless of payment).
3. **Paid services** (training, consulting brokerage, conference tickets) are priced at arm's length and open to non-members at a higher price.

## Non-profit guard-rails
- Non-privileged activity ≤ 10 % of total resources (§ 3a a). Track commercial revenue share quarterly.
- No distribution of surpluses; reserves for statutory projects (§ 3 c).
- Commercial operations may not compete with taxable businesses beyond what is unavoidable (§ 3a c): the association brokers consulting (§ 3 a 8) rather than selling it in competition with member firms.
- Administrative costs on donations ≤ 10 % if donation privilege is obtained (§ 3a j).
- Larger commercial activity (e.g. a DPP platform, § 3 a 14) belongs in a subsidiary (§ 3 a 9) approved by the General Assembly (§ 10 c 9).

## Cost assumptions (year 1)
Secretariat (part-time General Secretary, § 13 d): €45k · platform hosting and tools: €3k · conference: €30k (covered by fees and sponsorship) · legal/accounting/audit: €6k · travel to institutions: €6k.
